## 👋 About us

Solulever digitizes plant operations in the **process & batch manufacturing** industry — chemicals, specialty chemicals, agrochemicals and more — and simplifies the adoption of Industry 4.0 technologies so manufacturers can scale their operations and business.

Our mission: **make Digital Manufacturing programs simple, fast, fun & affordable.**

Solulever is a subsidiary of **[Taurus Group](https://tauruseu.com/)**. Together with our sister companies Cluster Vision and CircleB, we power Brabo with the infrastructure it needs, on the edge or in the cloud.

## 🧠 The Brabo Platform

Brabo goes beyond traditional IIoT software. It connects plant equipment to IT systems for full operational visibility, and combines edge and cloud computing with GenAI to turn contextualized manufacturing data into decisions.

| Capability | What it does |
|---|---|
| **OT Integration Bridge** | Plug-and-play drivers for leading PLCs and controllers (Siemens S7, Allen-Bradley MicroLogix/ControlLogix, Mitsubishi MELSEC) |
| **IT Integration Bridge** | Direct OT-to-IT integration with no middleware |
| **Build Your Own** | No-code SCADA Builder, apps, dashboards, KPIs, workflows and advanced analytics |
| **Complex Event Processing** | Real-time pattern detection, prediction and alerting across sensor streams |
| **Contextualization** | Manufacturing data enriched with context, ready for analytics and GenAI |
| **Brabo AIQ** | Conversational AI over contextualized plant data |
| **Cloud-agnostic** | Runs on AWS, GCP, Azure or private cloud, with a headless, microservices-based architecture |

## ⚡ Brabo at the Edge

- **Brabo Lite Appliance (BLA):** preinstalled OT integration bridge, data streamed to the cloud, 24-hour local backup against data loss.
- **Brabo Edge Appliance (BEA):** local platform historian for real-time and high-volume use cases, built for large plants, handling up to a million data points per second.
